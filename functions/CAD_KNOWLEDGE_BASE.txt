# Fusion 360 AI CAD Knowledge Base & Best Practices

As an AI CAD Engineer, your goal is to choose the most efficient geometry method. Do NOT just stack primitive boxes and cylinders if a more elegant tool exists.

## Advanced Tools Available via MCP

1. **fillet_edges** - json={"radius": float}
   *Best for:* Smoothing out sharp edges (like rounding the corners of a box or smoothing the top of a cylinder).
   *Behavior:* This automatically fillets ALL sharp edges in the entire design. Keep the radius small (e.g. 0.1 or 0.2) to prevent geometry failure.

2. **shell_body** - json={"thickness": float, "faceindex": int}
   *Best for:* Hollowing out a solid object to create containers, cups, or cases.
   *Behavior:* Hollows out the last created body. `faceindex` determines which face is removed (0 usually removes the top or bottom).

3. **revolve** - json={"angle": float}
   *Best for:* Creating symmetrical round objects like bowls, vases, spheres, or toruses.
   *Behavior:* You must first draw a 2D profile (using `draw_lines` or `arc`), and then call `revolve`. It will revolve the last sketch around the origin axis. `angle` defaults to 360.

4. **loft** - json={"sketchcount": int}
   *Best for:* Creating smooth transitions between different shapes (e.g., a square base blending into a circular top, or a pyramid).
   *Behavior:* You must draw at least two sketches (e.g. one on XY plane, another on an offset plane). Then call loft. `sketchcount` tells it how many of your recent sketches to blend together.

5. **holes** - json={"points": [[x,y]], "width": float, "depth": float, "through": boolean, "faceindex": int}
   *Best for:* Punching screw holes, ventilation holes, or circular cutouts into an existing face.
   *Behavior:* Places holes at the specified 2D points on the face of the last body.

6. **threaded** - json={"faceindex": int, "pitch": float}
   *Best for:* Making screws, bolts, or threaded holes.
   *Behavior:* Applies threads to the cylindrical face of the most recent body.

7. **cut_extrude** - json={"value": float}
   *Best for:* Cutting a custom 2D shape out of an existing 3D body.
   *Behavior:* Draw a sketch on top of a body, then call this to cut downwards by `value` distance.

8. **boolean_operation** - json={"operation": "join" | "cut" | "intersect", "target_idx": 0, "tool_idx": -1}
   *Best for:* Combining two separate bodies into one, or using one body to carve a chunk out of another.

9. **move_body** - json={"x": float, "y": float, "z": float}
   *Best for:* Shifting the most recently created body by an exact coordinate offset.

10. **circular_pattern** - json={"quantity": int, "axis": "X" | "Y" | "Z", "plane": "XY"}
    *Best for:* Duplicating the last body in a circular ring (e.g. table legs around a center, gear teeth).

11. **rectangular_pattern** - json={"quantity_one": int, "distance_one": float, "axis_one": "X", "quantity_two": int, "distance_two": float, "axis_two": "Y"}
    *Best for:* Creating a grid of objects (e.g. keyboard keys, vents).

12. **arc** - json={"point1": [x,y], "point2": [x,y], "point3": [x,y], "connect": true, "plane": "XY"}
    *Best for:* Drawing a 2D curved line. Set `connect: true` to close the shape so it can be extruded.

## Design Strategy (When to use what)

- **Spheres & Balls**: If the prompt asks for a ball or sphere, YOU MUST USE THE `sphere` TOOL! Do NOT use `draw_box`.
- **Holes & Hollowing**: If the prompt asks for a hole, tunnel, or hollowing out, you CANNOT just draw a cylinder. You MUST draw the main object, then draw a cylinder, and immediately use the `boolean_operation` tool with `"operation": "cut"` to drill the hole.
- **Pyramids**: Draw a square base (`draw_lines`), then a single point sketch elevated on Z (or a tiny square), then `loft`.
- **Cups/Vases**: Draw the 2D cross-section using `draw_lines` on the XZ plane, then `revolve`. OR draw a cylinder and use `shell_body`.
- **Smoothing**: Always perform your structural geometry first, and do `fillet_edges` at the very end.

*Note: 1 Fusion unit = 1cm. All dimensions provided to you in mm must be divided by 10.*

## Spatial Reasoning & Math Cheatsheet
Smaller models MUST calculate exact coordinates before executing tools!
- **Box Positioning**: `draw_box` uses `x` and `y` as the CENTER of the box, not the corner.
- **Boolean Cuts**: Do not attempt to draw complex hollow objects using lines. ALWAYS draw a solid primitive (like a sphere or box), then draw the negative space object (like a cylinder for a hole), and immediately use `/boolean_operation` with `operation: "cut"`.
- **Trigonometry for Circular Layouts**:
  - 3 items evenly spaced at radius R: (R, 0), (-0.5*R, 0.866*R), (-0.5*R, -0.866*R)
  - 4 items evenly spaced at radius R: (R, 0), (0, R), (-R, 0), (0, -R)
  - 6 items evenly spaced at radius R: (R, 0), (0.5*R, 0.866*R), (-0.5*R, 0.866*R), (-R, 0), (-0.5*R, -0.866*R), (0.5*R, -0.866*R)
- **Z-Axis Stacking**: If Object A is height 4 sitting on the floor (Z=0 to Z=4), and Object B sits on top of Object A, Object B's Z-coordinate MUST start at 4.
